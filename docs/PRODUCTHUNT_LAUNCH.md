## First Comment (Maker's Comment)

Hey Product Hunt! 👋

I'm excited to introduce **DAANSETU** - a platform to fight food waste and hunger.

**The Problem:**
- 40% of food produced in India goes to waste
- 190 million people go hungry daily
- Donors don't know where their food actually goes
- NGOs struggle to find reliable food sources

**Our Solution:**
We built DAANSETU (meaning "Bridge of Giving") with:

✅ **Government-verified NGOs** - Auto-verification via Darpan, 80G, MCA APIs
📍 **Real-time GPS tracking** - Watch your donation's journey
📸 **Photo proof** - See it being picked up AND delivered
🔐 **Complete transparency** - Full audit trail of every action

**Why Open Source?**
We believe fighting hunger should be a collaborative effort. Anyone can contribute, fork, or deploy their own version.

**Tech Stack:**
- Flutter (iOS + Android)
- Node.js + Express
- MongoDB
- Socket.IO

Would love your feedback! What features would help this reach more donors and NGOs?
